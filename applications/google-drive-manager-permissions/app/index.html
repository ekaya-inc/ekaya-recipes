Google Drive Manager

Who has access to your files

Scans your Google Drive, My Drive and every shared drive, and lists each other person with access and the files they can open. Scanning only reads names and sharing settings. Removing someone's access is a separate, confirmed step.

      Who opened your files

      From the Google Workspace audit log: every view, download and app read by someone other than you, grouped by person. It needs a Workspace administrator's audit-log credentials on the Google Workspace audit log connection.
